Pritesh Kakani

Engineer by training. Distributor by choice.

Pritesh Kakani is the founder of Kakani Wealth (registered business name: Kakani Financial Services) and an AMFI-Registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN-373766, EUIN E717134) and an APMI-Registered PMS Distributor (APRN10835). He holds a B.Tech and an M.Tech in Electronics from IIT Kharagpur (2014).

Before moving into financial services, he spent a decade as a software engineer, rising to Senior Software Engineer at companies including UnitedHealth Group, Nutanix, Qolsys, Vedantu and e-Zest Solutions. Alongside his engineering career, he has spent 9+ years actively following and participating in Indian stock and commodity markets. That background shapes how he works: structured, data-driven and process-first.

Today he helps resident and NRI families invest through mutual funds and other financial products, starting from their goals, their risk profile and their time horizon, with full transparency on costs and commissions. He is based in Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra, and works with clients across India and abroad.

Our Philosophy

Built on Intellect & Integrity

Most of an investor's long-term success comes from behaviour: staying disciplined, diversified and invested. Our job is to make that easier, and to add careful thinking where it matters.

🧠

Intellect

  • Portfolio over products. How your overall portfolio is built (allocation, diversification and goal mapping) matters more than picking any single scheme.
  • Active monitoring, not "buy and forget". Portfolios are reviewed and rebalanced as markets, schemes and your life change.
  • Tracking as a discipline. We use a structured, data-driven review process, shaped by an engineering background and 9+ years in the markets.
  • Risk management first. Protecting the downside comes before chasing returns: credit quality, concentration and liquidity are checked before anything else.
🤝

Integrity

  • Client first. Every suggestion has to serve your interest and suit your goals and risk profile.
  • No sales targets. We have no product-pushing incentives. If a product doesn't fit you, we won't suggest it.
  • Skin in the game. We only suggest products we understand and would be comfortable recommending to our own family.
  • Full disclosure. Commissions, costs, risks and direct-plan alternatives are always shared upfront.
