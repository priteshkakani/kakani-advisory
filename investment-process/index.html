Our Investment Process

Disciplined investing,
step by step

A structured, repeatable process, from understanding your goals to onboarding, implementation and ongoing monitoring, so you always know what we're doing and why.

Process Overview

Four stages, one goal: your long-term wealth

  1. 01

    Discovery Meeting

    We start with a conversation about your goals, income, existing investments, time horizon and comfort with risk, followed by a formal risk profile.

  2. 02

    Portfolio Design

    Each goal gets its own bucket. We suggest an asset allocation and shortlist suitable fund categories and schemes, with reasons, costs and commission disclosed.

  3. 03

    Implementation

    Once you approve, we set up SIPs and lump-sum investments in line with your risk profile and goals. Nothing is executed without your consent.

  4. 04

    Ongoing Monitoring

    We monitor your portfolio on an ongoing basis and review it formally every quarter, checking progress against each goal and suggesting rebalancing when needed.

Onboarding

Getting started is 100% paperless

Most investors are onboarded within a few working days, whether in India or abroad.

  1. 1

    Share Documents

    PAN, Aadhaar, a photograph, a cancelled cheque or bank proof, plus passport and overseas address proof for NRIs.

  2. 2

    KYC Verification

    Online KYC with a KYC Registration Agency (KRA), and FATCA declaration where applicable.

  3. 3

    Platform Registration

    We register you on an exchange mutual fund transaction platform so that investments can be placed online.

  4. 4

    Fund Selection

    Schemes are shortlisted against your goals, time horizon and risk profile. You approve the final list.

  5. 5

    SIP Setup

    Monthly SIPs are registered with an auto-debit mandate (e-NACH), so investing becomes automatic.

  6. 6

    Lump-Sum Investments

    One-time investments are placed directly, or through an STP if staggered entry suits you better.

  7. 7

    Portfolio Access

    You get online access to your holdings, plus your Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) from CAMS / KFintech.

  8. 8

    Quarterly Reviews

    Regular review meetings, with ongoing monitoring in between, to keep every goal on track.

Our Principles

What guides every recommendation

Discipline over timing

Consistent SIPs and staying invested matter more than predicting markets.

Goals first

Every rupee is mapped to a goal, a horizon and a suitable risk level.

Transparency

Clear reasoning, costs, commissions and direct-plan alternatives are always disclosed.

Your approval

You approve every strategy and transaction, and you can track your portfolio anytime.

Diversification

Spread across asset classes, fund houses and, where suitable, global markets.

Continuous review

Ongoing monitoring with formal quarterly reviews.

Investing takes time. Consistency and discipline are what keep investors focused on their long-term goals.
