Our Investment Process

Disciplined investing,
step by step

A structured, repeatable process, from the first meeting to onboarding, implementation and ongoing support, so you always know what we're doing and why.

Process Overview

Three stages, one goal: your long-term wealth

  1. 01

    Initial Meeting

    We discuss your goals, income, existing investments, time horizon and risk tolerance, complete a formal risk profile, and outline an initial strategy for each goal.

  2. 02

    Implementation

    Investments are matched to your risk profile and objectives. We share the suggested allocation and schemes with reasons, costs and commission, and execute only after your approval.

  3. 03

    Ongoing Support

    We monitor your portfolio continuously and review it formally every quarter against your objectives, suggesting rebalancing whenever it drifts or your life changes.

Onboarding

Getting started is 100% paperless

Most investors are onboarded within a few working days, whether in India or abroad.

  1. 1

    Document Submission

    Share basic documents: PAN, Aadhaar, a photograph and bank proof. NRIs also share a passport and overseas address proof.

  2. 2

    KYC Validation

    Your documents are verified and KYC is completed with a KYC Registration Agency (KRA), plus the FATCA declaration where applicable.

  3. 3

    Platform Onboarding

    You're registered on an exchange mutual fund transaction platform (NSE / BSE) so investments can be placed online.

  4. 4

    Fund Selection

    Schemes are evaluated against your objectives, time horizon and risk profile. You approve the final shortlist.

  5. 5

    SIP Setup

    Systematic Investment Plans are registered with an auto-debit mandate (e-NACH), so investing becomes automatic.

  6. 6

    Lump Sum Setup

    One-time investments are executed directly, or through an STP if staggered entry suits you better.

  7. 7

    Portfolio Access & Login

    You receive online access to your holdings, plus your Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) from CAMS / KFintech.

  8. 8

    Track Anytime

    View your portfolio online whenever you like. We guide you through it, with quarterly reviews and ongoing monitoring in between.

Our Principles

What guides every recommendation

Discipline over timing

Consistent SIPs and staying invested matter more than predicting markets.

Goals first

Every rupee is mapped to a goal, a horizon and a suitable risk level.

Transparency

Clear reasoning, costs, commissions and direct-plan alternatives are always disclosed.

Your approval

You approve every strategy and transaction, and you can track your portfolio anytime.

Diversification

Spread across asset classes, fund houses and, where suitable, global markets.

Continuous review

Ongoing monitoring with formal quarterly reviews.

Investing takes time. Consistency and discipline are what keep investors focused on their long-term goals.
