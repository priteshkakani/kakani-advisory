FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How often will my portfolio be reviewed?

We monitor portfolios on an ongoing basis and hold a formal review every quarter, or sooner if your goals or circumstances change.

Who approves the transactions in my portfolio?

You do. We share suggestions with the reasons and costs, and no transaction is placed without your approval. You can track your holdings online at any time.

Can you help me diversify globally?

Yes, through international mutual funds and fund-of-funds, within regulatory limits. We can also explain how the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) works for overseas investing.

Can you remove market volatility from my portfolio?

No one can. Market ups and downs are part of investing. Diversification, suitable asset allocation and a long time horizon can help you stay on track through them.

Do you give tax advice?

No. For tax matters, please consult a qualified tax professional or chartered accountant.

Who is Kakani Wealth?

Kakani Wealth is the customer-facing brand of Pritesh Kakani, trading as Kakani Financial Services, an AMFI-Registered Mutual Fund Distributor (ARN-373766, EUIN E717134) and APMI-Registered PMS Distributor (APRN10835) based in Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra. We help resident and NRI investors invest in mutual funds and other financial products.

Are you a SEBI-Registered Investment Adviser?

No. We are a mutual fund distributor. Any guidance we give is incidental to the distribution of mutual fund products. We do not charge advisory fees.

How do you earn? Are there hidden charges?

We are paid trail commissions by Asset Management Companies on regular plans. This is already built into the scheme's expense ratio, and there are no extra charges for you. While suggesting any scheme, we disclose the commission we receive from it and from competing schemes. Your half-yearly CAS also shows the actual commission paid in rupees.

What services do you offer?

Mutual fund distribution (SIP, lump sum, STP, SWP), goal-based investing incidental to distribution, and distribution of SIF, PMS, AIF, bonds and fixed deposits, subject to eligibility and the regulatory minimums.

How do I get started?

Book a call. We'll understand your goals and risk profile, complete KYC online, and set up your investments. Most clients are onboarded within a few days.

Is the process 100% online?

Yes. KYC, account opening, transactions and portfolio tracking can all be done online. In-person meetings are available in Ahilyanagar.

Can NRIs invest through you?

Yes. NRIs can invest through NRE or NRO accounts after completing KYC and FATCA. Some AMCs restrict investments from US and Canada residents, so we'll guide you to the schemes that accept them.

What is the minimum amount to start?

Many mutual fund SIPs start from ₹100–₹500 per month. SIF requires ₹10 lakh, PMS ₹50 lakh and AIF ₹1 crore as regulatory minimums.

Can I track my portfolio online?

Yes. You'll get access to online portfolio statements and consolidated account statements (CAS) from CAMS / KFintech.
