Overview

What is Specialised Investment Funds?

Specialised Investment Funds are a SEBI-regulated product category launched under the mutual fund framework. They are offered only by eligible AMCs, under a separate SIF brand, and are meant for investors who want differentiated strategies with the governance of a mutual fund.

Unlike regular mutual funds, SIF strategies may take limited unhedged short positions through derivatives and follow long-short or more concentrated approaches, within the limits SEBI prescribes.

At a glance

Minimum investment
₹10 lakh per investor (PAN level, across the SIF strategies of an AMC)
Suitable for
Experienced investors seeking strategies beyond a regular mutual fund, without a PMS-sized ticket
Typical horizon
Typically 3+ years, depending on the strategy
Liquidity
Varies by strategy. Redemption may be daily, weekly or at other intervals, as stated in the strategy's offer document

Options

Types & strategies

Equity Long-Short

Equity strategies that can take limited short positions to manage downside.

Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short

Long-short strategies focused beyond the largest 100 stocks.

Sector Rotation Long-Short

Rotation across sectors with limited short exposure.

Debt Long-Short

Fixed-income strategies with interest-rate positioning.

Sectoral Debt Long-Short

Debt strategies concentrated in chosen sectors.

Hybrid / Active Asset Allocator

Multi-asset long-short strategies across equity, debt and other assets.

How It Works

Ways to invest

Lump Sum

Invest the ₹10 lakh minimum, or more, in one go.

SIP / STP

Where the strategy allows, after the minimum threshold is met.

Redemption

As per the strategy's stated frequency and notice period.

Statements

Reported through your CAS, like mutual funds.

Key risks to understand

  • Derivative and short-selling risk can amplify losses.
  • Strategies are newer and have shorter track records.
  • Liquidity may be restricted compared with open-ended mutual funds.
  • Read each strategy's Investment Strategy Information Document carefully.
