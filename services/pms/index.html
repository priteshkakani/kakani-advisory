Overview

What is Portfolio Management Services?

Portfolio Management Services are offered by SEBI-registered Portfolio Managers. Unlike a mutual fund, the securities are held directly in your own demat account, so you can see every stock and every transaction.

PMS strategies are usually concentrated, often 15–30 stocks, and follow a defined investment approach such as growth, value, quality or small-cap. Fees may include a fixed management fee, a performance fee, or both, as set out in the PMS Disclosure Document.

Options

Types & strategies

Discretionary

The portfolio manager takes all investment decisions within the agreed strategy.

Non-Discretionary

The portfolio manager suggests trades, which are executed only with your approval.

Advisory

The manager only advises, and you execute the trades yourself.

Strategy Styles

Large-cap, multi-cap, mid & small-cap, thematic, value and quality approaches.

How It Works

Ways to invest

Funding

In cash, or by transferring existing stocks (subject to the manager's acceptance).

Fees

Fixed, performance-linked or hybrid, as disclosed upfront.

Reporting

Regular statements, plus performance published on the APMI website.

Top-ups

Additional investments as permitted by the agreement.

Key risks to understand

  • Concentrated portfolios can be more volatile than diversified funds.
  • Fees and taxes on churn can affect net returns.
  • Past performance of a strategy does not guarantee future results.
  • Read the PMS Disclosure Document and agreement before investing.
