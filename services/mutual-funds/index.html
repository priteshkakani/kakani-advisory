Overview

What is Mutual Funds?

A mutual fund pools money from many investors and invests it in equities, bonds, money-market instruments or gold, according to the objective stated in its Scheme Information Document. Each fund is managed by a SEBI-registered Asset Management Company (AMC), and investor money is held by an independent custodian and overseen by trustees.

Mutual funds give you diversification, professional management and daily liquidity with very small ticket sizes. They are among the most transparent investment products in India: NAVs are published daily and portfolios monthly.

Types & strategies

Equity Funds

Large, mid, small, flexi-cap, ELSS and sectoral funds for long-term growth.

Debt Funds

Liquid, money-market, short-duration, corporate bond and gilt funds for stability and income.

Hybrid Funds

Balanced advantage, aggressive and conservative hybrid, and multi-asset funds that mix asset classes.

Index Funds & ETFs

Low-cost passive funds that track indices such as the Nifty 50 or Sensex.

Solution-Oriented

Retirement and children's funds with a lock-in suited to long-term goals.

International / FoF

Fund-of-funds giving exposure to global markets, within regulatory limits.

How It Works

Ways to invest

SIP

Invest a fixed amount every month and benefit from rupee-cost averaging.

Lump Sum

Invest a one-time amount, such as a bonus or the proceeds of a sale.

STP

Park a lump sum in a debt fund and move it gradually into equity.

SWP

Withdraw a fixed amount every month, which suits retirement income.
